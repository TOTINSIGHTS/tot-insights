Leadership & loyalty

Named in every one of the 1,102 issues: the president as omnipresent patron.

Владимир Путин поручил Правительству России обеспечить устойчивую связь

Vladimir Putin instructed Russia’s government to ensure stable communications.

Кировск сегодня · Kadiivka · 2024-08-22

Putin appears as the provider of everything from internet to pensions, the single most frequent figure in the corpus.

Только вместе мы решим все поставленные перед нами задачи

Leonid Pasechnik: “Only together will we solve every task set before us.”

Красный Луч · Khrustalnyi · 2025-01-16

The installed LNR head’s loyalty refrain, the second most-quoted voice after Putin.

Passportisation & documents

Russian citizenship pressed onto residents; the paperwork machinery brought to every district.

С 1 января 2025 года лица без паспорта РФ будут обязаны…

From 1 January 2025, persons without a Russian passport will be obliged…

Труд горняка · Bryanka · 2024-11-15

Passportisation by pressure: residents without a Russian passport are reclassified as foreign nationals in their own towns.

машины для выездного обслуживания населения многофункциональным центром

…vehicles for mobile servicing of the population by the multifunction centre.

Антрацитовский вестник · Antratsyt · 2025-01-15

The administrative machinery of integration: mobile ‘MFC’ units pushing Russian documents into rural districts.

Children & militarisation

State youth structures and ‘patriotic’ war-games, mandated from Moscow and printed town to town.

«Семейная зарница»… для школьников и студентов… по указу Владимира Путина

‘Family Zarnitsa’ [a military war-game]… for schoolchildren and students… by decree of Putin.

5 papers · across the holding · Jan–Mar 2025

A military-patriotic programme for children, mandated by presidential decree, and printed identically in town after town, like the war reports.

«Движение Первых»… восполнения кадрового дефицита

‘Movement of the First’ [state youth movement]… to fill the personnel shortage.

Вестник Северодонецка · Sievierodonetsk · 2025-03-26

The Kremlin youth movement enlisted as a labour and cadre pipeline for the occupation administration.
