Eight days after Russia declared martial law in the four regions it claimed to annex, the occupation education ministry in Melitopol ordered its staff to stop using Ukrainian mobile numbers for work, including messaging apps registered to a Ukrainian number. In the autumn of 2022 many residents of occupied Zaporizhzhia still held Ukrainian numbers, and messengers registered to them were a main channel to colleagues, schools and relatives on the Ukrainian side. The order uses the martial-law decree as its basis and "the protection of information" as its stated purpose. It shows communications control applied first to the occupation's own administration, as part of moving the region onto Russian networks.

MINISTRY OF EDUCATION AND SCIENCE OF ZAPORIZHZHIA OBLAST (MON OF ZAPORIZHZHIA OBLAST)
ORDER
27 October 2022 · Melitopol · No. 23

On the protection of information

On the basis of Decree of the President of the Russian Federation No. 756 of 19 October 2022 "On the introduction of martial law in the territories of the Donetsk People's Republic, the Luhansk People's Republic, Zaporizhzhia and Kherson Oblasts", and for the purpose of protecting information,

I ORDER:

1. Employees of the Ministry of Education and Science of Zaporizhzhia Oblast shall exclude the use for work purposes of numbers of Ukrainian mobile operators, including the use of data-transfer programs in which the subscriber is registered by a number of a Ukrainian mobile operator.

2. I reserve control over the execution of this order.

Minister   E. A. Shapurova
