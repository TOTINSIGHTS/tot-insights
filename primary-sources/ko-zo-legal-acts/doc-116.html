Original Russian-language document. Regulations (Polozhenie) governing the organisation and activity of the Ministry of Education of Zaporizhzhia Oblast under the Russian Federation administrative framework. Issued by the occupation administration following the formal annexation of September 2022 and the integration of Zaporizhzhia Oblast into the Russian federal system. The document establishes the ministerial mandate, competencies, and organisational structure governing education across Russian-controlled parts of the oblast.

This document establishes the Regulations governing the Ministry of Education of Zaporizhzhia Oblast as a body of the Russian Federation occupation administration. The regulations set out the Ministry's mandate, its functions in supervising and licensing educational institutions, implementing federal state educational standards, overseeing the training and certification of teachers, managing preschool and school-age education, and coordinating the curriculum transition from the Ukrainian education system to the Russian federal system. The document is analytically significant for two reasons. First, it is the formal legal instrument institutionalising Russian education policy in occupied Zaporizhzhia: the Ministry created by these regulations is the body responsible for replacing Ukrainian school curricula with Russian federal standards, mandating Russian-language instruction, and eliminating Ukrainian-language education. Second, it documents the pace of administrative integration: within approximately two years of the initial occupation (2022), the education function had been transferred from improvised occupation arrangements into a bureaucratised ministry with a formal legal basis, budget lines, and hierarchical reporting structures indistinguishable from equivalent bodies in other Russian federal subjects.

General provisions

The Ministry of Education of Zaporizhzhia Oblast (hereinafter "the Ministry") is a body of executive authority of Zaporizhzhia Oblast exercising functions in the sphere of education. The Ministry operates in accordance with the Constitution of the Russian Federation, federal constitutional laws, federal laws, decrees and orders of the President of the Russian Federation, resolutions and orders of the Government of the Russian Federation, the Charter of Zaporizhzhia Oblast, laws and other regulatory acts of Zaporizhzhia Oblast.

Key functions

  • Implementation of federal state educational standards across all levels of general education in Zaporizhzhia Oblast.
  • Supervision, licensing, and state accreditation of educational organisations operating in Zaporizhzhia Oblast.
  • Oversight of the language of instruction in educational organisations, in accordance with federal legislation on the state language of the Russian Federation.
  • Organisation of teacher training, professional development, and certification in accordance with Russian federal qualification requirements.
  • Management of preschool, primary, basic, and secondary general education.
  • Implementation of additional education and supplementary educational programmes.

Analytical note — education as integration instrument

The function of "oversight of the language of instruction" in accordance with Russian federal legislation is the legal basis for the elimination of Ukrainian-language schooling in occupied Zaporizhzhia. Under Russian federal law, Russian is the state language; Ukrainian has no protected status. The Ministry created by these regulations is the institutional body through which the language, curriculum, and ideological reorientation of education in occupied Zaporizhzhia is administered. This regulatory basis connects to the broader education militarisation documented in the ZO collection and cross-referenced with the Mariupol education programme (DOC-099) and RF Federal Law No. 423-FZ (DOC-121).
