The original Russian-language order with the procedure it approves and two annexes, reproduced unaltered. In this copy the date and number of the order are left blank, so it is either the version circulated before registration or a draft; its internal deadlines run from 4 April to 12 July 2025. The file has been re-saved with document metadata removed.

The all-Russian verification tests (ВПР) are the Russian state's annual check of what pupils know against federal standards, run by Rosobrnadzor through its online system FIS OKO. This order applies them to every school in occupied Luhansk Oblast: pupils in all years except Years 1 to 3, 9 and 11 sit tests in subjects assigned at random by the federal organiser, with pupils coded by five-digit numbers, results uploaded to FIS OKO and analysed for the ministry by 12 July 2025. The procedure requires independent observers in schools, confidentiality rules for test materials and a ban on phones in the room. Annex lists name a regional coordinator and 23 municipal coordinators, one for each city and municipal district. They include the northern districts occupied only since 2022, such as Starobilsk, Novopskov, Bilovodsk and Troitske. The document shows the routine end of integration: schools in occupied Luhansk are graded in the same federal system and on the same Russian standards as schools in Russia, with the occupation ministry acting as a regional branch of Rosobrnadzor's testing machinery.

The order (pages 1 to 4)

Basis. Russian Government Resolution No. 556 of 30 April 2024 on the rules for assessing the quality of education; Rosobrnadzor Order No. 1008 of 13 May 2024 on the participants, timing and subjects of the VPR in 2024/2025; and the Regulations on the LNR Ministry of Education and Science approved by Decree of the Head of the LNR No. UG-64/23 of 13 June 2023.

Points 1 to 4. Conduct the VPR in the general education organisations of the LNR in 2025; approve the procedure; appoint a regional coordinator from the ministry's department of quality assessment; approve the list of municipal coordinators.

Point 5. The ministry's territorial bodies are to ensure that the tests are held under Rosobrnadzor Order No. 1008, appoint organisers and markers, ensure the "objectivity" of testing and marking, warn staff of their liability for breaching the confidentiality of test materials in FIS OKO, bring in independent observers and return the observers' reports by 20 May 2025.

Point 6. The LNR Regional Centre for Information Processing in Education trains the observers, keeps their reports for a year and sends the ministry a statistical analysis of the results by 12 July 2025.

Point 7. Heads of schools appoint organisers and markers (copies of their orders to the municipal coordinator by 4 April 2025), ensure every eligible pupil takes part, enter data in FIS OKO and admit observers on production of identity documents.

The procedure (pages 5 to 17)

1.1. The VPR monitor pupils' preparation against the federal state educational standards and federal basic educational programmes. «ВПР не требуют специальной подготовки обучающихся» ("The VPR require no special preparation of pupils").

1.2. Participants are pupils of accredited programmes, except those in Years 1 to 3, 9 and 11, pupils of closed special institutions and penal institutions, and of federal bodies' schools, including those of the Defence Ministry's "Era" military technopolis.

1.6. Phones and other devices are banned for organisers and pupils during the test; test materials may not be photographed or copied.

2.8. Subjects are assigned to classes at random by the federal organiser and released to the school in FIS OKO no earlier than seven days before the test.

3.1. The LNR Ministry of Education and Science is the regional coordinator; its territorial bodies are the territorial coordinators.

Annexes (pages 18 to 23)

Annex 1. Technical requirements for computer-based testing. Annex 2. The form of the independent observer's report on the conduct of the tests. List of municipal coordinators (pages 21 to 23): 23 officials of the ministry's territorial education departments, one for each city district and municipal district of the LNR, with their posts.
