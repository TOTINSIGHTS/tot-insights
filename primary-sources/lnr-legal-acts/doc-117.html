Correction, 18 November 2026. An earlier version of this page dated the law before February 2022 and described it as tightening exemptions from conscription in parallel with DPR Law No. 376-IINs. The act is Law No. 390-III of 22 June 2022, and it adds a ground relating to contract service in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation. TOT Insights corrected the page, its catalogue entry and its card on the Primary Sources page.

Original Russian-language document, one page. Law No. 390-III of the Luhansk People's Republic, signed by the Head of the LPR, L. I. Pasechnik, in Luhansk on 22 June 2022. It amends part 1 of Article 53 of LPR Law No. 241-II of 14 June 2018 "On Military Duty and Military Service".

Law No. 390-III adds one item to part 1 of Article 53 of the LPR military duty law: "16) in connection with entering military service voluntarily (under contract) in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation." The item opens with "in connection with", the form used in lists of grounds for leaving or ending military service; TOT Insights does not hold the text of Law No. 241-II and so cannot confirm what Article 53(1) lists. Pasechnik signed the law four months after the LPR declared general mobilisation in February 2022 (see the travel restrictions decree, DOC-118) and three months before the accession treaties of 30 September 2022. The item ties a provision of LPR military duty law to contract service in Russia's armed forces. It has no counterpart in DPR Law No. 376-IINs (DOC-107), which deals with mobilisation deferrals for students.

Title

Luhansk People's Republic. Law "On amending Article 53 of the Law of the Luhansk People's Republic 'On Military Duty and Military Service'".

Article 1

Amend part 1 of Article 53 of Law of the Luhansk People's Republic No. 241-II of 14 June 2018 "On Military Duty and Military Service" by adding item 16 as follows: "16) in connection with entering military service voluntarily (under contract) in the Armed Forces of the Russian Federation."

Article 2

This Law enters into force on the day following its official publication.

Head of the Luhansk People's Republic L. I. Pasechnik. Luhansk, 22 June 2022. No. 390-III.

Context

Other LPR acts on the site cover the departure ban on men liable for service during the 2022 general mobilisation (DOC-118) and the spring 2025 call-up decree issued after annexation (DOC-120). This law falls between them: it is dated 22 June 2022, after the general mobilisation and before the accession treaties of 30 September 2022, and it writes contract service in the Russian armed forces into the LPR military duty law.
