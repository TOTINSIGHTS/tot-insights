Decree No. 1181 of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, dated 19 June 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. It orders the liquidation of the Municipal Unitary Enterprise "Mariupolzhilkompleks" — the municipal housing-maintenance utility — and appoints a liquidator. Cross-reference: DOC-097 (subsidy amendment covering the same enterprise's wage arrears, January–February 2026).

Only months after subsidising its staff's unpaid wages (DOC-097), the occupation administration orders the municipal housing-maintenance enterprise "Mariupolzhilkompleks" to be wound up within one calendar year, appointing a liquidator to notify the tax authority, dismiss staff, settle creditors under Article 64 of the Russian Civil Code, transfer the enterprise's archives to a municipal storage centre, close its accounts and destroy its seals. Enacted under Russian corporate law (Federal Law No. 161-FZ on state and municipal unitary enterprises), the decree documents both the occupation administration's restructuring of Mariupol's municipal utility apparatus along Russian legal lines and the short-lived instability of the service entities it had itself created. On the Liquidation of the Legal Entity — Municipal Unitary Enterprise "Mariupolzhilkompleks" of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol of the Donetsk People's Republic

Decree No. 1181, 19 June 2026. Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic.

In accordance with the Civil Code of the Russian Federation; Federal Law No. 129-FZ of 08.08.2001 "On State Registration of Legal Entities and Individual Entrepreneurs"; Federal Law No. 161-FZ of 14.11.2002 "On State and Municipal Unitary Enterprises"; Federal Law No. 33-FZ of 20.03.2025 "On General Principles of the Organisation of Local Self-Government in a Unified System of Public Power"; Federal Law No. 131-FZ of 06.10.2003 "On General Principles of the Organisation of Local Self-Government in the Russian Federation"; the decision of the Mariupol City Council of 18.11.2024 No. I/21-3 (on the procedure for creating, reorganising and liquidating municipal enterprises and institutions); the decision of the Mariupol City Council of 20.01.2024 No. I/4-2 (on exercising the powers of the founder of municipal (communal) enterprises and institutions); and guided by the Charter of the municipal formation city district Mariupol and the Regulation on the Administration —

the Administration of the City District of Mariupol of the Donetsk People's Republic

DECREES:

1. To liquidate the legal entity — Municipal Unitary Enterprise "Mariupolzhilkompleks" (legal address: 287512, Donetsk People's Republic, city district Mariupol, city of Mariupol, Granitnaya Street, building 114; OGRN 1229300138924, INN 9310003840).

2. To set the liquidation term at up to the completion of all necessary procedures, but no more than one calendar year from the date of adoption of this decree.

3. To appoint as liquidator of the enterprise Pomazkov Mikhail Valeryevich.

4. To determine the legal address for the presentation of creditors' claims: 287512, Donetsk People's Republic, city district Mariupol, city of Mariupol, Granitnaya Street, building 114.

5. The liquidator shall, in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation, carry out the legal and organisational measures connected with the liquidation, including:

  • notifying the tax authority (within three working days) of the liquidation decision, the start of the liquidation procedure and the appointment of the liquidator, and obtaining confirmation of the corresponding entry in the Unified State Register of Legal Entities (EGRUL);
  • preparing the liquidator's work plan and publishing notice of the liquidation and the deadline for creditor claims in the "Herald of State Registration" and the Unified Federal Register of information on the activity of legal entities;
  • notifying the trade-union body (if any), the employment service, the statistics service, the Pension and Social Insurance Fund, and the enterprise's creditors and debtors;
  • warning employees of the forthcoming termination of employment at least two months in advance, personally and against signature, and subsequently dismissing them with the payment of compensation and the issuance of work-record books;
  • identifying creditors, recovering receivables, reconciling accounts with the tax and insurance-contribution authorities, and conducting an inventory of the enterprise's property;
  • drawing up the estimate of liquidation expenses, the interim liquidation balance sheet and the final liquidation balance sheet, submitting them to the Administration for approval, and settling creditors in the order of priority established by Article 64 of the Civil Code of the Russian Federation;
  • transferring the enterprise's archival documents to the municipal treasury institution "Mariupol Document Storage Centre";
  • closing the enterprise's accounts, submitting the application for state registration of the liquidation to the tax authority, and destroying the enterprise's seals and stamps.

6. The liquidator shall report the completion of the liquidation measures to the Administration of the City District of Mariupol.

7. The liquidation of the enterprise is considered complete, and the enterprise ceases to exist, after the entry of information on its termination in the Unified State Register of Legal Entities.

8. The Documentation Support, Organisational Support and Control Department of the Administration shall place this decree on the official website of the municipal formation (https://mariupol.gosuslugi.ru).

9. This decree enters into force on the date of its signing.

10. Supervision of the implementation of this decree is assigned to the Deputy Head of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Kalachev V.V.

Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov
