Original Russian-language document. Issued by the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, April 2026.

Decree authorising the electronic auction of two further non-residential municipal properties - Stroiteley Prospekt 39 (181.9 sq m) and Lenin Prospekt 151 (93.2 sq m) - on five-year leases under Russian federal law. Starting prices 2.07 and 1.32 million rubles per year. Auction organiser LLC "Kalmius." Documents the continuing transfer of Ukrainian municipal property into the Russian administrative and legal framework.
