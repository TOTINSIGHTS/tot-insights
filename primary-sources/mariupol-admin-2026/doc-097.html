Amendment Decree No. 728, 7 April 2026 - Key Operative Clauses

Subject: Amendments to Decree No. 677 of 31.03.2026 "On Approval of the Procedure for Providing a Subsidy from the Budget of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol of the Donetsk People's Republic for Financial Support (Reimbursement) of Costs Associated with Repayment of Wage Arrears for January–February 2026 of the Municipal Unitary Enterprise 'Mariupolzhilkompleks' of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol of the Donetsk People's Republic in respect of Powers for Maintenance of the Municipal Manoeuvring Housing Fund."

DECREES:

To amend Decree No. 677 of 31.03.2026 as follows:

  1. In the title of the Decree, replace "municipal manoeuvring fund" with "municipal manoeuvring residential fund"
  2. In the preamble, replace "municipal manoeuvring fund" with "municipal manoeuvring residential fund"
  3. In clause 1, replace "municipal manoeuvring fund" with "municipal manoeuvring residential fund"
  4. Clause 5 to read: "Supervision of implementation of this Decree shall be assigned to the acting head of the Housing and Communal Services Department of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol of the Donetsk People's Republic Krasnoludskaya T.Yu."

In the Procedure for Providing the Subsidy, replace "municipal manoeuvring fund" with "municipal manoeuvring residential fund" in clauses 1.1, 1.5, and 2.10.

The Documentation Support and Control Department shall place this Decree on the official website (https://mariupol.gosuslugi.ru).

This Decree shall enter into force on the date of its signing and applies to legal relations arising from 1 January 2026.

Supervision assigned to acting head of Housing and Communal Services Department Krasnoludskaya T.Yu.

Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol - A.V. Koltsov
