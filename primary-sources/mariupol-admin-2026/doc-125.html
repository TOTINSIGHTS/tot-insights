Original Russian-language document (scanned). Decree No. 1176 of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, dated 19 June 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. It amends Decree No. 1324 of 29.08.2025 — which defines the list of Mariupol administration officials empowered to draw up protocols on administrative offences — by restating the list (appendix) in a new edition, and repeals an earlier amending decree (No. 561 of 16.03.2026). Legal basis cited includes the Code of Administrative Offences of the Russian Federation, DPR Law No. 91-RZ of 08.07.2024 "On Administrative Offences", and DPR Law No. 99-RZ of 14.08.2024 delegating certain state powers to local self-government.

The decree documents the build-out of the occupation administration's administrative-enforcement apparatus in Mariupol — naming which municipal officials may issue penalty protocols for which categories of offence. The schedule ties named internal-policy officials to enforcement powers, including for "obstruction of the lawful activity of local self-government bodies and officials" (Article 7) and violations in the burial and funeral sphere (Article 11), among others. The inclusion of "obstruction of local self-government" as an enforceable administrative offence is notable in an occupation context. Decree No. 1176, 19 June 2026 — On Amendments to Decree No. 1324 of 29.08.2025

1. To amend Decree No. 1324 of 29.08.2025 "On Determining the List of Officials of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol Authorised to Draw Up Protocols on Administrative Offences", setting out its appendix in a new edition (attached).

2. To repeal Decree No. 561 of 16.03.2026 (a prior amendment to Decree No. 1324).

3.–5. The decree directs publication on the official portal (https://mariupol.gosuslugi.ru), enters into force on the date of signing, and assigns supervision of implementation to the Deputy Head of Administration, Polunina M.V.

Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov

Appendix — Officials authorised to draw up protocols (extract)

The appendix is a schedule mapping articles of the DPR administrative-offences law to the municipal officials empowered to draw up protocols. Among the entries visible on the scan:

  • Article 7 — Obstruction of the lawful activity of local self-government bodies and their officials (failure to provide, or providing distorted/incomplete, information; obstructing their lawful actions): Head of the Internal Policy Department of the Administration (Vovchenko G.O.); Deputy Head of the Internal Policy Department (Kulikov E.V.).
  • Article 11 — Violations in the sphere of burial and funeral affairs: Chief Specialist of the Department for Beautification and the Organisation of Road-Traffic Works.
