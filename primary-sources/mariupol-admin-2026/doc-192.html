Mariupol City Administration Acts, April to August 2026: 18 Decrees and Orders of the Occupation Administration

Mariupol, occupied Donetsk Oblast

Format

Language

The occupation administration of Mariupol issues numbered decrees (postanovleniya) and orders (rasporyazheniya) on the running of the city. TOT Insights holds 25 of them from 2026, published here on 18 document pages: the five decrees creating territorial self-government bodies share one page, as do the three orders listing "ownerless" property. Each page carries the Russian original and an English translation or the key provisions in English.

The decrees cover four areas of the administration's work. It builds Russian-model city government, from territorial self-government bodies to a list of officials entitled to write administrative-offence reports. It manages the property left by the siege: official housing valuations, lease auctions of municipal buildings, repairs of war-damaged flats, and three lists of homes and premises that show "signs of ownerless property", the first step towards taking them into municipal ownership. It courts business, through an investor-support procedure and a small-business programme. And it sets programmes for schools and young people. As routine instruments of local government, the acts record how the occupation administers Mariupol day to day.
