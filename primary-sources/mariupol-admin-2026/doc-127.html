Decree No. 1178 of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, dated 19 June 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. It approves the Regulation on the review of investor inquiries and the accompaniment of investment projects being implemented and/or planned on the territory of Mariupol. Legal basis cited includes Federal Law No. 39-FZ of 25.02.1999 (investment activity), Federal Law No. 160-FZ of 09.07.1999 (foreign investment), Order of the RF Ministry of Economic Development No. 672 of 26.09.2023 (the "Regional Investment Standard"), and DPR Law No. 444-IINS of 17.05.2023 on investment policy.

The decree documents the occupation administration establishing an investment-facilitation apparatus in Mariupol modelled directly on Russia's "Regional Investment Standard" (RF Ministry of Economic Development Order No. 672 of 2023). By setting up a formal procedure to receive investor inquiries and "accompany" investment projects — including foreign investment — it is part of integrating occupied Mariupol into the Russian investment-promotion system and courting capital into the occupied city. Decree No. 1178, 19 June 2026

On the Approval of the Regulation on the Review of Investor Inquiries and the Accompaniment of Investment Projects Being Implemented and/or Planned for Implementation on the Territory of the Municipal Formation City District of Mariupol of the Donetsk People's Republic.

For the purpose of implementing the policy of attracting investment into the economy of the municipal formation, and in accordance with the federal investment legislation and the "Regional Investment Standard":

1. To approve the Regulation on the review of investor inquiries and the accompaniment of investment projects being implemented and/or planned on the territory of the city district of Mariupol (attached). The Regulation sets out the procedure by which the Administration receives and reviews inquiries from investors and provides organisational "accompaniment" of investment projects in the occupied city.

The decree directs publication on the official portals and enters into force accordingly. Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov.
