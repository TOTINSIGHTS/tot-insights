Five decrees of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, all dated 18 June 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. They establish "territorial public self-government" (TOS) bodies — Russian-law neighbourhood self-organisation structures — across Mariupol districts. Four decrees (Nos. 1162, 1163, 1164, 1165) approve the boundary schemes for the territories of the planned bodies "Ellinsky", "Kalmiussky", "Krasnoarmeysky" and "Rodnoy Dom"; one (No. 1166) registers the charter of the body "Magnolia". Legal basis cited includes Federal Law No. 33-FZ of 20.03.2025 and DPR Law No. 235-RZ of 12.12.2025 "On the Organisation of Local Self-Government in the Donetsk People's Republic".

Within a single day the occupation administration issued five decrees rolling out a network of Russian-style "territorial public self-government" (TOS) bodies across Mariupol's neighbourhoods — the mechanism, drawn from Russian municipal law, by which residents' self-organisation is channelled into structures whose boundaries and charters are approved and registered by the occupation administration. The district labels include Soviet-era names such as "Krasnoarmeysky". Taken together, the decrees document the granular extension of the Russian local-governance template into Mariupol's residential districts. Decrees Nos. 1162–1165 (18 June 2026) — Approval of TOS territory boundaries

Each of the four decrees follows the same operative form. On the approval of the scheme of the boundaries of the territory on which it is planned to carry out territorial public self-government:

  • No. 1162 — "Ellinsky";
  • No. 1163 — "Kalmiussky";
  • No. 1164 — "Krasnoarmeysky";
  • No. 1165 — "Rodnoy Dom".

1. To approve the scheme of the boundaries of the territory on which it is planned to carry out territorial public self-government (attached).

2. The relevant department of the Administration shall place this decree on the official website of the municipal formation (https://mariupol.gosuslugi.ru).

3. This decree enters into force on the date of its signing.

Decree No. 1166 (18 June 2026) — Registration of the charter of TOS "Magnolia"

1. To register the Charter of the territorial public self-government "Magnolia", adopted by the conference of citizens on the organisation of territorial public self-government "Magnolia" (attached).

2. The Digitalisation and Communications Directorate of the Administration shall publish this decree in the State Information System of Regulatory Legal Acts of the Donetsk People's Republic (https://gisnpa-dnr.ru).

Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov
