Decree No. 1179 of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, dated 19 June 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. It amends the municipal programme "Development of Youth Policy in the Municipal Formation City District of Mariupol for 2026–2028" (approved by Decree No. 1753 of 14.11.2025).

The decree documents the occupation administration extending Russia's federal youth-policy and patriotic-ideological infrastructure into Mariupol. The revised programme aligns a Mariupol "Youth Centre" with the recommendations of the Russian Federal Agency for Youth Affairs (Rosmolodezh), targets drawing 75% of youth aged 14–35 into youth-policy activities by 2028, funds the programme at roughly 93.8 million rubles across 2026–2028, and rewrites its activities to include "preventive work with youth at the place of residence", support for volunteers, and the "creation and dissemination of anti-terrorist content" — framing that, in an occupation context, casts resistance as terrorism while channelling young people into a Russian ideological programme. Decree No. 1179, 19 June 2026 — On Amendments to the Youth Policy Programme (Decree No. 1753 of 14.11.2025)

1. Amendments to the programme passport:

  • Co-implementer: the Municipal Budgetary Institution of Youth Policy of the Administration, the "Youth Centre" ("MBU MP AGOM ‘MTs’").
  • Task: to increase the share of the municipal formation's youth aged 14 to 35 involved in youth-policy activities to 75% by 2028, including on the basis of the newly created youth-policy infrastructure.
  • Expected result (added): "by 2027, one youth centre created and equipped in accordance with the recommendations of the Federal Agency for Youth Affairs".
  • Projects: "Creation and development of the Youth Centre of the city district of Mariupol".
  • Financing: total 93,818.60326 thousand rubles (2026 — 73,818.60326 thousand; 2027 — 10,000 thousand; 2028 — 10,000 thousand rubles).

2. Amendments to the text of the programme (Section 3 restated), setting out a municipal project "Creation and development of the Youth Centre" and a complex of process measures "Organisation and holding of events in the sphere of youth policy", which include: development and implementation of youth-event plans; organising city youth events; preventive work with youth at the place of residence; encouraging youth who show special success; support measures for volunteers; information support; and the creation and dissemination of anti-terrorist content. A further complex provides for the participation of the district's youth in events held outside the district.

The decree directs publication on the official portals, enters into force on the date of official publication, assigns coordination to the youth-policy department (Aksyutina V.A.), and states that the Head retains control. Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov.
