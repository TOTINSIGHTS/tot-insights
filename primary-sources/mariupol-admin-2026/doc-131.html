Decree No. 821 of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, dated 22 April 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. Issued in connection with personnel changes, it amends Decree No. 261 of 25.02.2025, which approved the Regulation on the commission for assigning (changing) names to elements of the street-road network and planning-structure elements on the territory of Mariupol; it restates the commission's composition (Appendix 1). Legal basis cited includes Federal Law No. 443-FZ of 28.12.2013 (the federal information address system) and RF Government Decree No. 1221 of 19.11.2014 on assigning, changing and annulling addresses.

The decree documents the administrative machinery through which the occupation administration controls toponymy — the naming and renaming of Mariupol's streets, roads and planning-structure elements. Control of place-names is a core instrument of identity replacement in occupied territory (the Soviet-era district name "Krasnoarmeysky" seen in DOC-123 illustrates the direction of travel); this decree keeps the naming commission staffed following personnel changes. Decree No. 821, 22 April 2026 — On Amendments to Decree No. 261 of 25.02.2025

Issued in connection with personnel changes in the Administration:

1. To amend Decree No. 261 of 25.02.2025 "On Approval of the Regulation on the Commission for Assigning (Changing) Names to Elements of the Street-Road Network and Elements of the Planning Structure on the Territory of the Municipal Formation City District of Mariupol": in Appendix 1, the composition of the commission for assigning (changing) names to elements of the street-road network and planning-structure elements is restated.

The decree directs publication on the official portals and enters into force accordingly. Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov.
