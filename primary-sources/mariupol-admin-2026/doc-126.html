Decree No. 1177 of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol, Donetsk People's Republic, dated 19 June 2026, signed by the Head of Administration, A. V. Koltsov. Issued in connection with personnel changes, it approves the Regulation on the commission for compliance with the requirements for the official conduct of the Administration's municipal servants and for the resolution of conflicts of interest, together with the commission's composition. Legal basis cited includes Federal Law No. 273-FZ of 25.12.2008 "On Combating Corruption", Federal Law No. 25-FZ of 02.03.2007 "On Municipal Service", and Presidential Decree No. 821 of 01.07.2010.

The decree transposes the Russian federal anti-corruption and official-conduct framework onto Mariupol's municipal service, establishing the standing commission that reviews conflicts of interest and breaches of the conduct requirements by municipal servants. It documents the occupation administration adopting Russian administrative-integrity machinery — modelled explicitly on the federal template (Presidential Decree No. 821 of 2010) — as it builds out its municipal apparatus. Decree No. 1177, 19 June 2026

On Approval of the Regulation on the Commission for Compliance with the Requirements for the Official Conduct of Municipal Servants of the Administration of the City District of Mariupol Holding Municipal-Service Posts, and for the Resolution of Conflicts of Interest.

Issued in connection with personnel changes in the Administration, and in accordance with the federal legislation on municipal service and on combating corruption:

1. To approve the Regulation on the commission for compliance with the requirements for the official conduct of municipal servants … and for the resolution of conflicts of interest (appendix 1).

2. To approve the composition of the commission (appendix 2).

The decree directs publication on the official portals and enters into force on the date of official publication. Head of the Municipal Formation of the City District of Mariupol — A. V. Koltsov.
