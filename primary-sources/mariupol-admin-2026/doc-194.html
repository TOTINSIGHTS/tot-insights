Mariupol City Administration Decrees, 8 to 20 May 2026 (Nos. 918 to 960): Lease Auctions, Demolition Orders and Administrative Measures

Nine numbered decrees (postanovleniya) of the occupation administration of Mariupol, signed between 8 and 20 May 2026. They continue the series published in the Mariupol city administration collection (DOC-192). Dates and numbers are handwritten on the originals and were read from the scans.

Four of the nine concern municipal property. Decree 918 and the documentation approved in Decrees 936 and 937 put five-year leases on two bath and sauna complexes, a ground-floor catering space on Lenina Street and two premises first offered in April (Decree 722) up for auction, at starting rents from about 1 million to 14 million roubles a year. Decree 960 declares 11 apartment blocks on Moskovskyi and Tulskyi lanes and Vokzalna Street "unsafe and subject to demolition" and their flats unfit for habitation. The other five regulate outdoor advertising and signboards, paid services by municipal utility institutions, the renaming of the property directorate and internal procurement control.

Decree No. 918 · 8 May 2026

Lease auction: two bath complexes and premises on Lenina Street

Orders an electronic auction of five-year leases on three municipal properties, run by LLC "Kalmius": a bath and sauna complex at Mitropolytska Street 5 (1,036.2 sq m), a second complex at 25th Kvartal 13 (1,019 sq m) and ground-floor premises at Lenina Street 68 for catering (1,170.5 sq m). Starting rents for the two complexes are 10,142,327.16 and 9,715,486.80 roubles a year, with deposits of 20 per cent and a bidding step of 5 per cent.

Decree No. 935 · 18 May 2026

Advertising structures moved because of a "city case"

Approves a list of advertising structures to be moved locally, away from the approved placement scheme, for up to 12 months because of a "city case" (gorodskoy sluchai), and defers a decision on two more: a billboard on Naberezhna Street and a city-board on Pryazovska Street.

Decree No. 936 · 18 May 2026

Auction documentation: the bath complexes and Lenina Street 68

Approves the auction documentation for the three lots of Decree 918 on the RTS-tender electronic platform. Starting rents: 10,142,327.16 roubles a year (Mitropolytska Street 5), 9,715,486.80 (25th Kvartal 13) and 14,343,522.29 (Lenina Street 68); five-year leases, subletting allowed.

Decree No. 937 · 18 May 2026

Auction documentation: premises on Lenin and Metallurgov Prospekts

Approves the auction documentation, on the RTS-tender platform, for the two premises first offered in Decree 722 (DOC-096): 74.6 sq m at Lenin Prospekt 153 and 165.8 sq m at Metallurgov Prospekt 235, at starting rents of 1,053,650.40 and 1,887,209.94 roubles a year.

Decree No. 938 · 18 May 2026

Paid services by municipal housing and utility institutions

Approves regulations on paid services by municipal budget institutions in housing and utilities, and allows the street-lighting, roads and parks institutions (Mariupolgorsvet, Mariupolavtodor and Zelenstroy) to charge for services within their main activities.

Decree No. 947 · 18 May 2026

Property and Land Relations Directorate renamed a Department

Renames the administration's Directorate of Property and Land Relations as the Department of Property and Land Relations and orders its re-registration with the federal tax inspectorate.

Decree No. 951 · 19 May 2026

Plan for internal control of procurement in 2026

Approves the 2026 plan for the administration's internal (departmental) control of procurement by the bodies it oversees, and the officials who carry it out.

Decree No. 957 · 20 May 2026

Procedure for removing unlawful signboards

Approves a procedure for dismantling information structures (signboards) installed or used in breach of the placement rules in Mariupol.

Decree No. 960 · 20 May 2026

Eleven apartment blocks declared unsafe and subject to demolition

On the basis of 11 conclusions of the interdepartmental housing commission dated 20 May 2026 (Nos. 627 to 637), declares 11 apartment blocks "unsafe and subject to demolition" and their flats unfit for permanent habitation: Moskovskyi Lane 9, 15 and 19; Tulskyi Lane 38, 42, 44, 45, 47, 48 and 50; and Vokzalna Street 31, building A.
