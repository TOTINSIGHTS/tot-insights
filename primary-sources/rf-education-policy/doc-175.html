The original Russian-language letter of the Deputy Minister of Education, No. OK-1799/03 of 22 June 2026, reproduced in full with its 27-page annex. It asks the regions to pass three lists on to schools "for use in stocking school libraries with fiction" and in teaching the subjects Literary Reading and Literature. The lists are works included in the federal educational programmes, works recommended for extracurricular reading, and works "of patriotic orientation created by contemporary writers". The letter notes that the third list was first sent on 14 August 2025 (letter No. OK-2287/03). The file has been re-saved with document metadata removed; its content is unaltered.

The third list grades contemporary "patriotic" books by age: 6+ for primary school, 12+ for Years 5 to 9 and 16+ for Years 10 and 11. Its sections run from medieval princes and the "commanders of Holy Rus'" to the Second World War. For Years 5 to 9 and for Years 10 and 11 alike, it ends with a section titled in the Latin-letter "Z" of the war against Ukraine: «Zа наших», "for our guys". The eight books under that heading include Shadows of Donbas: Small Stories of a Big War, Heroes of the SVO: Symbols of Russian Courage, Donbas, the Heart of Russia and an anthology of poems about the war. Elsewhere the lists add books on Sevastopol and Crimea as "Russian", a children's book on how Admiral Ushakov "made the Black Sea Russian", Vladimir Medinsky on the "myths of the USSR" and Zakhar Prilepin. Neither the programme list nor the two reading lists contains a single Ukrainian author: there is no Shevchenko, Lesya Ukrainka or Franko, and the word "Ukraine" does not appear. Because the letter is about stocking school libraries, these lists also shape what children in occupied schools can find to read.

Note

A search of the full text finds no work by Taras Shevchenko, Lesya Ukrainka or Ivan Franko in any of the three lists, and the word "Ukraine" (Украина) does not occur. Nikolai Gogol, a Russian-language writer born in the Poltava region, appears in the programme list.
