Three original Russian-language documents of the Russian Ministry of Education, reproduced in full. They are the Concept of Historical Enlightenment in Educational Organisations, approved by the Ministry's collegium on 15 April 2026, together with its implementation plan for 2025–2030. The second is the guidance On organising historical enlightenment in general-education organisations in the 2026–2027 school year, which is undated and does not name its issuer in the text. The third is the Deputy Minister's letter No. OK-1984/03 of 9 July 2026 to every regional education authority, sending a methodological letter on extracurricular activities. The files have been re-saved with document metadata removed; their content is unaltered.

The three documents set up a school-based system of "historical enlightenment" (istoricheskoe prosveshchenie) that applies in every Russian school, including those in occupied Ukraine. The Concept presents history teaching as an instrument of "worldview sovereignty" against "unfriendly" foreign states. It names the war against Ukraine in its rationale, lists an "ideological" function among the functions of historical enlightenment, and makes the involvement of "participants and veterans of the special military operation" a line of work in schools. It also proposes an expert council at Moscow Pedagogical State University to examine the most influential online history sources and prevent the spread of "false" history contrary to Russian values. Among its named partners is the "New Chersonesus" museum and church complex in occupied Sevastopol. The 2025–2030 implementation plan sets numeric targets, including history lessons on the "feat" of Second World War and SVO participants for more than 50,000 people a year and wider pupil volunteering to "support the heroes of the SVO". The 2026–2027 guidance turns this into a 0–18 point rating for each school, three of whose indicators are compulsory for every school. The July 2026 letter puts it into the timetable. It adds new historical-enlightenment courses for Years 2 to 11 alongside the weekly "Conversations about What Matters" and recommends "military training camps / basic military training" of 17 hours in Year 8 and 35 hours in Year 10.

1. Concept of Historical Enlightenment in Educational Organisations (approved 15 April 2026)

About 34 pages of concept followed by an implementation plan for 2025–2030 that names responsible bodies, deadlines and targets. Delivery partners named in the plan include the Russian Military Historical Society, the Russian Historical Society, the Znanie society, the Volunteers of Victory movement, the "Russia: My History" history parks, the Presidential Library and the Fund for Humanitarian Projects.

2. Guidance on organising historical enlightenment in the 2026–2027 school year

3«…необходимо разработать планы мероприятий по реализации Концепции и обеспечить информационную поддержку путем создания специальных страниц на официальных сайтах…»
"…it is necessary to develop action plans for implementing the Concept and to provide information support by creating special pages on official websites…"		Addressed to the regions.
5«…популяризации среди обучающихся разных возрастов вопросов из истории развития культуры, науки, техники, военно-промышленного комплекса, военной истории России…»
"…popularising among pupils of different ages questions from the history of culture, science, technology, the military-industrial complex and the military history of Russia…"		Content that schools are to add beyond the history curriculum.
11–14«Рекомендуемые «ключевые» показатели определяют состав позиций, выполнение которых является обязательным для каждой общеобразовательной организации.»
"The recommended 'key' indicators define the items whose fulfilment is compulsory for every general-education organisation."		Six indicators, each scored 0–3, give a school 0–18 points: "basic" (6), "medium" (7–12) or "high" (13–18). The three compulsory ones are at least one special format such as a club or lecture series, at least three historical-enlightenment events a year, and a dedicated section on the school website.
