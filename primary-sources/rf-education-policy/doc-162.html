Four original Russian-language ministerial orders reproduced from the official legal information portal, and the annexed list of literary works included in the federal programmes of primary, basic and secondary general education. Order No. 769 approved the federal list of textbooks admitted for use in schools; Order No. 258 amended annexes 1 and 2 of that list; Order No. 495 re-approved the whole list in June 2025. Order No. 436 of 22 June 2026 (registered by the Ministry of Justice on 22 July 2026, No. 87563) re-approved the list again, repealed Order No. 495 and the three orders that had amended it, and is the version in force. The literature list (8 pages, 219 authors) accompanies the federal educational programmes.

The federal textbook list determines what may be taught in any Russian school, including those in the occupied territories, because only textbooks on the list may be used. The list under Order No. 436, like its predecessors, carries native-language and native-literature textbooks for Bashkir, Tatar, Adyghe, Chechen and other languages of the Russian Federation, but no title in or on the Ukrainian language. The literature list for the federal programmes likewise contains no Ukrainian-language work. Order No. 436 is also the first list to admit the regional history textbooks Donbass and Novorossiya: History of Our Region for the occupied territories (published in full as DOC-171). Russian law formally allows parents to request instruction in a native language "within the possibilities of the education system", but with no admitted textbook, no teacher-training track and no examination, the route is unusable for Ukrainian. The four orders are therefore evidence that the exclusion of Ukrainian from occupation schools requires no prohibition: the menu of admitted materials simply omits it. They are cited in TOT Insights research on education under occupation and on de-Ukrainianisation as institutional substitution.

Order No. 769 of 5 November 2024

Approves the federal list of textbooks admitted for use in the delivery of state-accredited programmes of primary, basic and secondary general education (annex 1) and the list of textbooks for the languages and literatures of the peoples of Russia (annex 2). Establishes the period for which earlier textbooks may continue in use.

Order No. 258 of 1 April 2025

Amends annexes 1 and 2 of Order No. 769: adds and removes titles, updates publishers and editions. It did not approve a new list.

Order No. 495 of 26 June 2025

Re-approves the full federal list of textbooks (331 pages) and repeals Order No. 769 as amended. Annex 1 lists textbooks by subject and grade; the native-language section includes titles for Bashkir, Tatar, Adyghe, Chechen, Yakut and other languages of the Russian Federation. No entry is in or about the Ukrainian language. Amended by Orders No. 768 (27 October 2025), No. 973 (18 December 2025) and No. 203 (26 March 2026); repealed by Order No. 436.

Order No. 436 of 22 June 2026

Signed by Minister S. S. Kravtsov; registered by the Ministry of Justice on 22 July 2026 (No. 87563) and published on the official portal on 23 July 2026. Annex 1 approves the new federal list of textbooks; Annex 2 sets the final dates until which textbooks excluded from the list may still be used. The order repeals Order No. 495 and its three amending orders. As before, the native-language section covers Bashkir, Tatar, Adyghe, Chechen and other languages of the Russian Federation, and no entry is in or about the Ukrainian language.

Annex 1 admits, as items 566 and 567 (codes 1.1.2.9.30.1 and 1.1.2.9.30.2), the textbooks Istoriya nashego kraya. Donbass i Novorossiya for Year 5 and for Years 6 to 7 by A. P. Bogdanov and V. N. Ilyin, each with the accompanying visual aid for Years 5 to 7 (printed page 112 of the annex). The Ministry of Education itself is listed as the rights holder, the publisher field is left blank, and the expert approval on which admission rests has no expiry date. Unlike neighbouring regional-history entries, which cite their earlier admission under Order No. 495, these two carry no earlier admission: Order No. 436 is the order left blank in the imprints of the books (see DOC-171).

Federal literature list

The annexed list of literary works included in the federal programmes runs to 219 numbered authors, from Abramov to Yakovlev, with the works set for study. It contains Russian, Soviet and world literature in Russian translation; no work is in Ukrainian.

Analytical note

Under Russian law a school may use only admitted textbooks. In the occupied territories the list is therefore not a recommendation but the boundary of the possible: a school need ban nothing to teach nothing Ukrainian.
