Donetsk People's Republic: Law No. 376-IINs of 20 May 2022 on Mobilisation Deferral for Students and Graduates (Amending Article 23 of the Law on Military Duty and Article 17 of the Law on Mobilisation)

Correction, 18 November 2026. An earlier version of this page dated the law before February 2022 and described it as narrowing exemptions from conscription in preparation for the general mobilisation. The act is dated 20 May 2022 and adds deferrals from mobilisation for students and graduates. TOT Insights corrected the page, its catalogue entry and its card on the Primary Sources page.

Original Russian-language document, 11 pages: a print of the act's page on the DPR legal acts portal, saved on 5 June 2026. The People's Council adopted Law No. 376-IINs on 20 May 2022 and D. V. Pushilin signed it in Donetsk the same day. It amends two laws: Article 23 of the Law on Military Duty and Military Service (No. 08-INs of 13 February 2015; see DOC-105) and Article 17 of the Law on Mobilisation Preparation and Mobilisation (No. 10-INs of 17 February 2015; see DOC-106).

Law No. 376-IINs adds deferrals from call-up for mobilisation for students and recent graduates. Pushilin declared general mobilisation in the DPR on 19 February 2022 (DOC-108), and this law followed three months later, while the mobilisation remained in force. Article 2 adds four items to Article 17(1) of the mobilisation law: full-time pupils and students at accredited schools, colleges, universities and university preparatory departments, for the length of their programme (item 7); postgraduate, residency and assistantship students, with up to one further year to defend a dissertation (item 8); graduates who pass the state final examinations, until 1 October of that year, and master's graduates until they start a doctoral, residency or assistantship programme (item 9); and full-time students at licensed religious seminaries (item 10). Article 1 writes the same graduate provision into Article 23 of the Law on Military Duty. The law widens the grounds on which young men in occupied Donetsk Oblast could defer mobilisation. Its text gives no reason for the change and no figure for the number of people it covered.

Title

Law of the Donetsk People's Republic "On amending Article 23 of the Law of the Donetsk People's Republic 'On Military Duty and Military Service' and Article 17 of the Law of the Donetsk People's Republic 'On Mobilisation Preparation and Mobilisation in the Donetsk People's Republic'". Adopted by resolution of the People's Council on 20 May 2022.

Article 1

Amend Article 23 of Law of the Donetsk People's Republic No. 08-INs of 13 February 2015 "On Military Duty and Military Service" by restating item (g) of part 2 as follows: "(g) persons who have successfully passed the state final examination for: the programme of secondary general education, secondary vocational education, or a bachelor's or specialist programme, until 1 October of the year in which they pass that examination; a master's programme, until the start of study on a postgraduate (adjunct), residency or assistantship-internship programme in the year in which they pass that examination;".

Article 2

Amend Article 17 of Law of the Donetsk People's Republic No. 10-INs of 17 February 2015 "On Mobilisation Preparation and Mobilisation in the Donetsk People's Republic" by adding items 7 to 10 to part 1 [the list of persons granted deferral from call-up for military service by mobilisation]:

Item 7. Persons studying full-time at: (a) organisations teaching state-accredited secondary general education programmes, for the period of study within the standard duration; (b) organisations teaching state-accredited secondary vocational programmes, where the student had not completed secondary education before enrolling, for the period of study and until the age of 20; (c) the same organisations, where the student had completed secondary general education before enrolling and reaches call-up age in the final year, for the period of study; (d) state higher education institutions, for study in a preparatory department funded from the republican budget, for no more than one year, where the student enrolled in the year of completing secondary education; (e) state-accredited bachelor's, specialist and master's programmes, where the student holds no degree at that level, for the standard period of study.

The deferral under item 7 is granted once only, with three exceptions that allow a second deferral as a student moves up a level: from school to a preparatory department; from school or a preparatory department to a bachelor's or specialist programme; and from a bachelor's to a master's programme. A student keeps the deferral on academic leave, on transfer to another programme of the same level, or on reinstatement at the same institution (except after expulsion by the institution), provided the total period of deferral grows by no more than one year, or not at all on reinstatement.

Item 8. Persons studying full-time on state-accredited postgraduate (adjunct), residency or assistantship-internship programmes, for the period of study and for the defence of the qualifying work (dissertation), but no more than one year after completing the programme.

Item 9. Persons who have successfully passed the state final examination, on the same terms as Article 1: until 1 October of that year, or for master's graduates until the start of a postgraduate, residency or assistantship-internship programme in the same year.

Item 10. Persons studying full-time on secondary or higher vocational programmes that train clergy and religious personnel at licensed religious educational organisations, for the period of study within the standard duration.

Article 3

This Law enters into force on the day following its official publication.

Head of the Donetsk People's Republic D. V. Pushilin. Donetsk, 20 May 2022. No. 376-IINs.

Context

The law sits in a sequence of four DPR acts on the site: the Law on Military Duty (DOC-105), the Law on Mobilisation Preparation and Mobilisation (DOC-106), Pushilin's general mobilisation decree of 19 February 2022 (DOC-108), and this amendment of 20 May 2022. Article 17(1) of the mobilisation law ran to item 6 before this amendment; items 7 to 10 extend deferral to students, graduates and seminarians. The text records the rules only. It does not state whether students had received call-up notices during the first three months of the mobilisation, and TOT Insights holds no figures on how many people the deferral covered.
