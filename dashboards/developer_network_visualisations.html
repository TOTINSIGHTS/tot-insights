Four Ownership Clusters and Nine Additional Companies in Occupied Territory

Each cluster groups construction companies operating in occupied territory (amber) with their corporate parent or holding structures (teal). Ownership links are expressed at corporate level per registry records. The Etalon Group cluster - a major listed St Petersburg developer with an occupied-territory subsidiary structure routed through AO RSK and OOO SFO Direct Finance - is analytically the most significant structural finding.
