Russian Developer Networks in Occupied Ukraine: Corporate Ownership Map

Last updated: May 2026

Interactive OSINT investigation tracing construction and developer companies operating in occupied Donetsk and Zaporizhzhia to their beneficial owners. Multiple nominally independent companies share common ownership structures, geographic origins, and in several cases individuals with accountability flags.

Four Ownership Clusters Operating in Occupied Territory

Each cluster groups one or more construction companies in occupied territory (amber) with their controlling owners and directors (blue = beneficial owner, red = individual with accountability flag, teal = holding structure, gray = director). The Etalon Group cluster — a major listed St Petersburg developer with a covert occupied-territory subsidiary structure — is analytically the most significant finding.
