TONNAGE NOTE  ·  The 8,000t/vessel figure applies to documented bulk cargo (coal, ore, kaolin) under the Autumn 2024 operational model. At 10–20 vessels/month this implies a monthly throughput of 80,000–160,000 tonnes. The Coal Harbor dredging tender (May–October 2026) is designed to increase vessel capacity beyond this ceiling — exact post-dredge capacity not yet specified.
The Temryuk Re-Registration Mechanism
The March 18 shipment is the first explicitly documented instance. One vessel, cargo unspecified in tonnage.
Dual-track capacity note: The November 2025 direct customs post enables clearance without Temryuk — for cargo that can be openly declared (coking coal to Turkey, wheat to Egypt). The Temryuk route handles cargo whose occupied-territory origin must be concealed. These are complementary mechanisms, not alternatives. Post-dredging (from Q4 2026), both tracks will have higher vessel capacity.
