Lisichansk Occupation Press Archive: Вестник Лисичанска and Лисичанский вестник (2023)

DOC-E1 · Information Space · Primary Sources · TOT Insights

Occupation propaganda archive, primary sources: This page presents a partial archive of occupation-administration newspapers published in and for occupied Lisichansk, Luhansk region. These are primary sources, reproduced for research and documentation. Their content is Russian occupation propaganda and does not represent the views of the TOT Insights Hub or its contributors. They are made available so researchers, journalists, and the public can study how the occupation administration presents itself to residents of occupied territory.
Holdings note
This is a partial archive, not a complete run.

Title A (Вестник Лисичанска): 10 issues held (No. 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 30). Missing: issues 2 to 10, issue 19, and issues 21 to 29.

Title B (Лисичанский вестник): 1 issue held (No. 1). No later issues currently held.
