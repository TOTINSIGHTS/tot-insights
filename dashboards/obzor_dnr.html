DPR Ombudswoman: Social-Humanitarian Situation Reports

Source note: These reports were produced by the Ombudswoman of the self-proclaimed Donetsk People's Republic (DPR) and are primary sources from within the occupation administration. They should not be treated as reliable accounts of conditions in Russian-occupied Donetsk. Their analytical value lies in what they acknowledge - the complaints and grievances the DPR apparatus considered acceptable to record. Read critically and against independent sources.
