Resistance Activity: Monitoring Dashboard
25 Dec 2024 – 8 Oct 2026 · 555 violent incidents · 909 non-violent acts · Occupied territories and Russian Federation
Methodology

These incidents are gathered by monitoring more than 40 online Ukrainian and Russian sources to identify violent resistance acts committed by Ukrainian partisans inside the occupied territories or Russia, or by Ukrainian-connected or Ukrainian-run partisan groups inside Russia. The sources are not shared, so as not to draw attention to them; some have small reach or are private. We triangulate the events identified against occupation sourcing and/or other Ukrainian sources before including them. Nevertheless, these cases must be treated with caution, and as indicative rather than representative.

Incidents are logged in fortnightly reports. For this edition every incident was re-coded by location, type and claiming group, and eight incidents that had been logged in two consecutive fortnights were counted once. Group attribution is as claimed by the group or the reporting channel.

What changed

Violent resistance has moved out of the occupied territories and into Russia. Recorded incidents in occupied Ukraine averaged about 7 per fortnight in the first half of 2025 and 9 in the second half, then fell to about 3.5 per fortnight in 2026 and to between 1 and 5 since May 2026. Incidents in Russia rose from about 3 per fortnight in early 2025 to between 7 and 11 for most of 2026, and have outnumbered occupied-territory incidents in every fortnight since late December 2025 bar one.

Total activity dipped in June to August 2026 (7 to 11 incidents a fortnight) and recovered to 12 to 15 in September and early October, mostly in Russia.

Violent incidents by type
Reading the types
Each incident is given one type from its description. Rail sabotage (relay cabinets, locomotives, fuel tank wagons) is the largest category and dominates activity inside Russia. "Other" covers arson against buildings, offices and volunteer collection points, symbolic attacks and incidents whose target is not described.

Violent incidents by claiming group
Who operates where
ATESH (with OTPOR) claims the most incidents, and nearly two thirds of its claims are in Russia. The Freedom of Russia Legion's resistance network, Black Spark and Rospartizan operate almost only inside Russia. Antismersh and S.R.O.K. operate only in the occupied south and east, and Mariupol Resistance mostly in occupied Donetsk oblast. "Unattributed" covers incidents reported by news or regional channels without a group claim.
Non-violent resistance acts per quarter

Leafleting, graffiti, ribbons and flags, symbolic actions, leaks of occupation documents and public defiance, logged from June 2025. Quarters are approximate: the log records each act's report, and dates were reconstructed from the order in which reports were published.

Non-violent acts by reporting group
Non-violent resistance
Non-violent acts outnumber violent incidents by about two to one over the same months. They rose through late 2025 to a peak in early 2026 and have fallen since, by about 40% from the first to the third quarter of 2026. ATESH reports almost half of all recorded acts.
