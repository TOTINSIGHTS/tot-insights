Source note: Ten documents gathered by TOT Insights in April 2026, nine in Russian and one in English. Three come from bodies operating in occupied territory or address it directly: the Crimean "House of Friendship of Peoples" (D4-4), Rosmolodezh's manual for the four annexed regions (D4-6) and the occupation Ministry of Youth Policy of Zaporizhzhia Oblast (D4-7). The reading lists and the competition guide are federal and apply across Russia, and the three books were published in Moscow. The copies rarely say where or how they were used, so each description below states what the document says about itself.

Correction, 18 November 2026. Earlier versions of this page attributed D4-4 to a federal body, described D4-1 as a recommended list for occupied territories, called D4-9 a state manual and gave D4-10 a different title, publisher and year. TOT Insights corrected these entries against the documents.
Reading lists
3 documents
A federal school literature list, a children's reading list and a youth library's list of the most-requested books. All three apply across Russia, including the occupied territories.
Youth and education guidance
4 documents
Guidance for teachers, cultural workers and youth officials. Three of the four come from or for the occupied territories: occupied Crimea (D4-4), the four regions annexed in 2022 (D4-6) and occupied Zaporizhzhia Oblast (D4-7).
Books on Ukraine and the war
3 documents
Three books published in Russia: allegations against Ukrainian forces (D4-8), a guide to Ukrainian information operations as its author describes them (D4-9) and an English-language history of Ukraine for foreign readers whose authors include a former LPR foreign minister (D4-10).
