Research, data, and primary sources on Russian-occupied Ukraine

The home of research on Ukraine’s temporarily occupied territories. TOT Insights brings together analytical briefings, interactive data tools, primary source collections, and curated datasets - the central resource for evidence-based analysis of Russian occupation mechanisms, civilian entrapment, and accountability.
