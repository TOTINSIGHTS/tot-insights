Projects

TOT Insights hosts dedicated project pages for multi-output research strands, funded investigations, and advisory engagements. Each project brings together analytical outputs, datasets, and team information for a specific research line.

Active projects and engagements
Active 2026 –
Youth Militarisation in Occupied Ukraine
Examines the mechanisms of youth militarisation across Russia’s occupied territories, with particular focus on extracurricular and paramilitary programmes within Russian nationalities strategy, counter-extremism frameworks, and occupation education policy.
Funder
Leverhulme Centre for Research on Slavery in War
Active 2026 –
Governing War at the Frontier: Occupied Territories, Russian Strategic Decision-Making, and Regime Stability
A one-year RSI project investigating how Russia governs occupied territories, how frontier conditions shape strategic decision-making in Moscow, and what this reveals about regime stability and future conflict trajectories.
Funder
RSI
Active 2026 –
Settler Colonialism in Occupied Ukraine
A team-led project with IKAR colleagues investigating the motivations driving Russians to relocate to occupied Ukrainian territories and the implications for scholarly definitions of settler colonialism.
Funder
Centre for Statecraft and National Security, King’s College London
Completed 2025–2026
Lessons from the Ukrainian Resistance
A commissioned essay for the Secretary of State drawing on original fieldwork and interviews with resistance members to extract lessons from Ukraine’s experience of occupation for UK and allied national defence planning.
Funder
SONAC
