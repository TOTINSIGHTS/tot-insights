Contact

TOT Insights is a research platform on Russia's occupation of Ukrainian territory, based in the Ukraine & Russia Programme at the Centre for Statecraft and National Security, King's College London. We welcome correspondence from researchers, policymakers, journalists, and prospective partners, in English, Ukrainian, or Russian.
