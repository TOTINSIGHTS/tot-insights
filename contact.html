What your support enables
01
Analysis that informs policy

Western Europe faces urgent decisions about Ukraine, Russia, and long-term security — decisions that require accurate, granular understanding of what Russian occupation actually does to territories and populations. TOT Insights produces the analytical infrastructure those decisions depend on.

02
Primary sources, not approximations

Our fieldwork inside occupied territory, systematic monitoring of occupation administrative records, and open-source data collection produce evidence that does not exist elsewhere. This is not analysis at a distance — it is documentation from as close to the ground as it is possible to get.

03
Reaching the people who need it

Our research has been cited in Hansard, used by the UN Commission of Inquiry on Ukraine, and briefed directly to legislators and policymakers across the UK, Norway, Sweden, Estonia, and the United States. Sustained support is what allows us to keep producing it and getting it to the right audiences.

Work with us

TOT Insights operates across two tiers. Our open-access research — briefings, datasets, dashboards, and primary source collections — is published freely on this platform. We also undertake commissioned analytical work for government bodies, international organisations, and institutional partners where outputs are delivered privately rather than published.

We welcome approaches from foundations and government bodies interested in supporting the platform. We are also open to research partnerships, joint projects, and data-sharing agreements with academic institutions, think tanks, and accountability organisations working on related questions.

If you would like to discuss any of the above, please get in touch directly.

TOT Insights was established with seed funding from the Leverhulme Centre for Research on Slavery in War, King's College London.
