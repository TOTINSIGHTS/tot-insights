Help us document what occupation does to people.

What your support enables
01
Field research and monitoring

Original fieldwork in Ukraine and systematic monitoring of occupation administrative records, commercial registries, and open-source data — the primary source base on which the platform's analytical outputs depend.

02
Analytical briefings and data tools

Peer-reviewed publications, analytical briefings, and interactive dashboards translating complex occupation data into accessible formats for researchers, policymakers, and accountability practitioners.

03
Policy engagement

Direct briefings to legislators and policymakers in the UK, Norway, Sweden, Estonia, and the United States, and contributions to UN accountability mechanisms — ensuring that research findings reach the audiences that can act on them.

Work with us

TOT Insights is a collaborative platform and we are always open to discussing ways of working together. We welcome approaches from researchers, research institutions, journalists, documentary makers, and policymakers who are working on questions related to Russian-occupied Ukrainian territories, occupation governance, civilian entrapment, or accountability.

We are particularly interested in partnerships involving data sharing, joint research, or co-production of analytical outputs. We have established working relationships with partner institutions in Ukraine, across Europe, and in North America, and are open to new collaborations that strengthen the evidential base for understanding occupation and its consequences.

If you are working on a funded project or research programme with relevant overlap, or if you have access to primary source material from Russian-occupied territories, we would be glad to hear from you. Please use the contact details below to get in touch.
