Theme overview

Social Conditions Under Occupation

Russian occupation has fundamentally disrupted civilian life across occupied Ukrainian territories - displacing hundreds of thousands, destroying housing and cultural institutions, persecuting religious communities, and implementing demographic replacement programmes. TOT Insights documents the mechanisms and human consequences of occupation across Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson, Luhansk, and Crimea.
