Theme overview

Economics of Occupation

Russia's occupation of Ukrainian territory has been accompanied by systematic economic extraction — the seizure of agricultural land, industrial facilities, energy infrastructure, and financial systems. TOT Insights documents the institutional mechanisms, business networks, and international chains of complicity sustaining the economic architecture of Russian-occupied Ukraine.
