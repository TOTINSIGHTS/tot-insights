Closed Monitoring

TOT Insights Hub access policy on restricted material

The TOT Insights Hub maintains a closed monitoring stream covering occupied-territory developments that we do not publish openly. This page explains why we withhold certain materials from the public site, and how vetted organisations may request access.

Our duty to our contributors

We withhold certain material to protect the safety of people in occupied territories and to meet our ethical and data-protection obligations. Making some information publicly available could endanger people living under occupation and expose those who have contributed to our work.

Where our public briefings draw on this restricted material, we cite it as "TOT Insights closed monitoring" or, where relevant, "Centre for the Study of Occupation monitoring," the latter being one of our partners.

Requesting access

Access to restricted material may be granted on a case-by-case basis to applicants who can demonstrate a legitimate professional need and the ability to handle the information securely.

To request access, contact jade.mcglynn@kcl.ac.uk, including your organisation, your role, your professional mandate relating to the occupied territories, and the purpose of your request.

We may ask for further information before reaching a decision. All requests are subject to our data-protection obligations and our existing ethics approval.
